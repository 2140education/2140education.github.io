Our Mission

At 2140 Education Pty Ltd, our mission is to provide inclusive, individualised, and empowering education and support services that enrich the lives of learners and individuals living with disability.

We believe everyone—regardless of background, ability, or circumstance—deserves access to quality education and holistic care.

Homeschooling Services

We specialise in supporting families who choose education outside of mainstream systems. Through academic mentoring, curriculum customisation, and capability-building for parents and carers, our homeschooling services offer flexible, learner-centred pathways.

We put each student's strengths and needs at the centre.

NDIS Support

As an NDIS provider, we deliver programs designed to align with participants' goals while building long-term independence.

From capacity-building and life skills coaching to educational therapy and community participation, we help individuals not only meet their NDIS objectives but thrive beyond them.

Why We Exist

Standardised systems leave too many behind.

At 2140 Education, we create space for the possible—designing education and support that adapts to the individual, rather than forcing individuals to adapt to the system.

Our work is guided by trauma-informed practice and a neurodiverse-affirming philosophy. By partnering with families, caregivers, and support networks, we ensure that learning and care environments are safe, adaptable, and grounded in respect for each person's lived experience.

The Vision: 2140

The name 2140 reflects our long-term vision: a future where all individuals—gifted learners, those with additional needs, and people with complex requirements—are supported to live purposeful, connected lives.

We are committed to creating environments that foster belonging, agency, and growth.
