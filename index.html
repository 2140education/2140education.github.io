Empowering Learning. Enabling Lives.


At 2140 Education Pty Ltd, our mission is to provide inclusive, individualised, and empowering education and support services that enrich the lives of learners and individuals living with disability. We believe that every person—regardless of their background, ability, or circumstance—deserves access to quality education and holistic care.

Founded with a passion for learner-centred approaches, 2140 Education operates at the intersection of homeschooling innovation and NDIS service delivery. We work closely with families, caregivers, and support coordinators to provide flexible, trauma-informed, and neurodiverse-affirming learning pathways tailored to each persons needs. Our homeschooling support services include academic mentoring, curriculum customisation, and capability-building for parents and carers who are choosing to educate their children outside of traditional schooling environments.

In our capacity as an NDIS provider, we offer a range of supports aligned with participants’ goals and aspirations. These include capacity-building programs, life skills coaching, educational therapy, and community participation activities. Our aim is to not only help NDIS participants achieve their stated goals, but to go beyond – empowering them to flourish in education, independence, and social engagement.

The name 2140 represents our long-term vision: a future where all individuals—regardless of perceived limitations—are fully supported in living purposeful, connected lives through tailored learning and care. Whether working with gifted learners, those with additional needs, or individuals with complex support requirements, our team is committed to fostering belonging, agency, and growth.

We exist because we know that one-size-fits-all systems fail too many. At 2140 Education, we create space for the possible.
